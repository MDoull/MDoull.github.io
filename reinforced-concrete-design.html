Column, Slab and Beam Design

This project involved the complete design of continuous reinforced concrete beams/slabs/columns for a multi-story office building using Eurocode 2 standards. I performed load calculations, structural analysis, flexural and shear design, and reinforcement detailing for a four-span continuous beam system. The work demonstrates my ability to apply structural design codes in practice, make engineering judgments on member sizing and reinforcement selection, and communicate technical design decisions through professional drawings and calculations. Awarded a First Class grade for thorough analysis and clear presentation of results.
