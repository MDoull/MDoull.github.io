EWB: Sheffield Wind Turbine Project

This extracurricular interdisciplinary engineering project involved designing and constructing a 2.4m diameter wind turbine for Whirlow Farm as part of a 30-student collaborative team. Working on the mechanical design team as the structural engineer, I developed detailed 3D models in AutoCAD to validate the turbine design and support fabrication. The project required close coordination across electrical, mechanical, and civil engineering disciplines, translating theoretical knowledge into practical application. This hands-on experience demonstrates my ability to work effectively in large, diverse teams and apply structural principles to real-world renewable energy systems.
