Python: Embodied Carbon Optimisation

This project demonstrates my ability to solve real-world sustainability challenges through computational optimization. I developed a Python tool that minimizes embodied carbon in truss structures, achieving up to 5% carbon reductions through geometric optimization. The solution showcases my capability to translate complex engineering requirements into efficient, data-driven solutions that deliver measurable environmental and economic value.

Technical approach: Calculated member forces and cross-sectional areas based on 350 MPa yield stress. Parametric analysis across 1000+ configurations. Bisection optimization converging in 18 iterations. Validated through force equilibrium checks.
